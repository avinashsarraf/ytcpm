## Short description

YTCPM is a free browser-based toolkit for YouTube earnings scenarios and report-based CPM/RPM calculations. Explore audience-country, niche and format assumptions, or enter matching report totals to calculate a metric. Sources, formulas and limitations are published. No signup or private-channel connection is required.

## Longer listing description

YTCPM helps creators distinguish earnings scenarios from actual report metrics. Its homepage models long-form ad earnings using views, CPM, ad fill and stated revenue-share assumptions. A separate Shorts scenario uses an engaged-view basis and clearly labeled assumptions.

The dedicated CPM calculator uses gross advertiser revenue or spend and ad impressions. The RPM calculator uses creator revenue and matching views; it does not subtract a platform share from creator revenue a second time. Guides explain how to check your own metrics in YouTube Studio and compare consistent reporting periods.

The current calculators run in the browser without a user account. Country/niche ranges and currency conversions are directional planning inputs, not verified global averages or a live feed. The site cannot retrieve another channel's private CPM from a public URL and does not provide authenticated channel monitoring. The Usage Monitor concerns the website's calculation API, not connected YouTube reports.

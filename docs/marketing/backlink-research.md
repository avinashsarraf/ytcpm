## What the research established

1. Google explicitly identifies automated link building, low-quality directory links, excessive exchanges and paid links passing ranking credit as link-spam patterns. Useful discovery is different from manufacturing links solely to manipulate rankings. [1](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)
2. Moz says DA is its comparative metric, not a Google ranking factor. PA is also a Moz score, not a Google metric. [1](https://moz.com/learn/seo/domain-authority) [10](https://moz.com/learn/seo/page-authority)
3. A normal unqualified link does not need `rel="dofollow"`. Inspect the actual anchor for `nofollow`, `sponsored` and `ugc`; paid placements should be qualified. Publication, crawling, indexing and ranking credit are different things. [3](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links)
4. Generic directory lists conflict with official rules. BetaList's current support says submissions are paid; its eligibility page requires a genuinely new product. [4](https://betalist.com/support) [1](https://betalist.com/criteria)
5. Peerlist says not every launch gets a backlink: its FAQ says only the weekly top five. A verified individual profile and complete project are needed. [6](https://help.peerlist.io/individual/launchpad/frequently-asked-questions-about-spotlight-by-peerlist) [3](https://help.peerlist.io/individual/launchpad/how-to-launch-a-project-on-peerlist-launchpad)
6. Uneed's own help describes score-based retention conditions and a queue. Do not treat its free launch as an unconditional dofollow placement or manipulate votes. Check the live form again before committing to a launch date. [1](https://help.uneed.best/getting-started/how-to-submit-your-first-product)
7. The fetched Startup Stash listing page currently shows **“This typeform is now closed”**: https://startupstash.com/add-listing/. This is a blocked prospect, not a completed submission.
8. The fetched Fazier submission page requires a reciprocal homepage/footer link for its free tier and advertises paid backlink packages: https://fazier.com/submit. No reciprocal link was added to YTCPM.
9. PitchWall's fetched homepage emphasizes AI products: https://pitchwall.co/. YTCPM uses deterministic calculations; **do not pretend it is an AI product** just to qualify for AI directories.
10. Publishing is not a loophole for automated promotional articles. Hashnode prohibits SEO abuse/bulk promotion. DZone explicitly rejects promotional material and lists AI-generated submissions as a suspension cause. Do not submit this AI-prepared kit there. [3](https://hashnode.com/code-of-conduct) [4](https://dzone.com/articles/dzones-article-submission-guidelines)

## First actions: relevance before metrics

**A** = my recommended first review, not a measured authority score. **B** = relevant but conditional. **C** = lower priority or policy research needed. **HOLD** = a concrete current blocker/risk; not an actionable approved opportunity.

Start with **SaaSHub, AlternativeTo and Launching Next** for an accurate product listing, followed by a properly planned **Product Hunt** launch. Use actual owner identity and only real alternative categories. For editorial work, start with the directly relevant **Buffer, Shopify and HubSpot** articles; locate an official editorial contact and offer a specific reader benefit. No cold-email campaign has been started. A published relevant reference can be useful even when its link is qualified; do not reject good audience exposure solely because it is nofollow.

**No paid options authorized.** Do not buy ranking credit, promise reciprocal links or add badges to YTCPM without a separate decision. A pay-to-promote placement must be assessed as advertising, with proper link qualification, not as guaranteed SEO authority.

## Verification workflow for a real placement

1. Confirm the product fits the platform and that the owner controls the account. Read current terms, editorial rules and AI-content rules. Check whether an existing YTCPM listing already exists to avoid duplicates.
2. Confirm current cost, queue and any badge/reciprocity conditions. Do not authorize payment or reciprocal links as part of this research.
3. Submit once using accurate product copy, authentic screenshots and the real owner contact. Record the actual submission receipt and date. “Form submitted” is not “approved.”
4. Only after publication, record the exact public listing/article URL. Verify a real HTML anchor to the intended YTCPM page, its `rel`, and any redirect chain. `noopener`/`noreferrer` are not synonyms for `nofollow`.
5. Check actual response, canonical, meta robots/X-Robots-Tag, crawler access and whether the link is hidden behind login. A 200 response or absence of noindex is not proof of indexing; a `site:` query's absence is also not definitive.
6. If needed, record Moz DA for the domain **and PA for that exact live page**, tool/export and measurement date. Never transfer the homepage's PA to a new listing. Recheck the anchor instead of trusting a directory's marketing claim.
7. Update the CSV only with evidence: `SUBMITTED_PENDING_REVIEW`, `PUBLISHED`, `REJECTED` or `REMOVED`, live URL, date and observed attributes. Keep dofollow/qualified/no-clickable-link statuses distinct. Inspect the complete page as well as the anchor before describing it as an unqualified placement.
8. Measure qualified referral visits and calculator use; use the owner's Search Console to review links/indexing where available. Do not promise traffic, DA increases, rankings or AI recommendations.
